Privacy Policy

This policy covers PocketCoach (also called Pocket Coach) for iOS and Android, developed by Alexander Povolotskiy. The app works without an account, advertising, or analytics. Your practice records and movement analysis are processed locally rather than uploaded to us.

Information stored on your device

The app can store match details, player or opponent details you enter, marked errors, scores, notes, optional video links, and squat workout summaries including repetition metrics and form feedback. The iOS version also has local coach-review previews. This information is kept in the app's local storage on your device; there is no developer-operated cloud synchronization or account database.

Camera and movement analysis

Squat Coach requests camera permission for live squat practice. On iOS, Apple's on-device Vision framework identifies body-joint positions. On Android, CameraX supplies camera frames to a bundled Google MediaPipe pose-detection model running on the device. Frames and detected joint positions are processed in memory to count repetitions and provide general form cues. PocketCoach does not record, save, upload, or share camera frames or movement video. Saved workouts contain summaries and repetition metrics, not camera footage. Camera processing stops when the session is paused, closed, or the app leaves the foreground. You can deny or revoke camera permission in your device settings and still use non-camera match tracking.

Optional spoken cues

On iOS, spoken cues use Apple's installed on-device voices. On Android, cue text and repetition counts are passed to the installed Android text-to-speech service using an available offline English voice. PocketCoach excludes voices that report requiring a network connection and does not initiate voice downloads or fall back to a network voice. If no eligible voice is available, spoken coaching is unavailable. Camera frames, video links, and match notes are not sent to the speech service. The installed speech engine is a separate system or third-party component governed by its provider's policies.

Information we collect

PocketCoach does not send your camera data, practice records, personal information, or usage analytics to the developer or to advertising or analytics services. It does not sell personal information or track you across apps or websites. The Android CameraX and MediaPipe components are used for local camera processing and pose detection, not a cloud coaching service. No microphone, contacts, location, or health-record access is needed for these features.

Sharing you choose

If you choose to share a workout summary, PocketCoach passes the text summary to the destination app you select through the system share sheet. This can include workout date, repetition count, form scores, and feedback. The receiving app or recipient handles that copy under its own policies. PocketCoach does not automatically send summaries to the developer or a coach, and deleting the original does not delete shared copies.

Video links and coach-review preview

Entering a video link stores a reference locally; it does not upload the video or the link to us or automatically analyze the video. The iOS coach-review area is a local preview only: it does not process payments or send information to a coach. No paid human-coaching service is provided by the Android version covered here.

Local security, retention, and backups

Records remain in the app's private local storage until you delete them or remove app data. Protection depends on your device's operating-system security, screen lock, and access controls; PocketCoach does not add a separate encrypted vault. We do not retain a server copy. On iOS, operating-system backups may contain app data according to your backup settings. The Android app disables app backup in its manifest; device-manufacturer transfer or backup behavior can vary. Keep your device secure and review any device backups separately.

Data removal

You can delete individual matches and saved workouts in the app. On Android, Clear storage in the app's system settings or uninstalling the app removes its local records and preferences. On iOS, deleting the app removes its local data, subject to any retained operating-system backups. Shared summaries, external videos, and any device backups are separate copies that you must manage with the relevant recipient or provider. There is no PocketCoach account or server-held practice history to delete.

Support messages and this website

If you voluntarily email apov@me.com, your email address and message are handled through email providers to respond to your request. Include only information needed for support and avoid private videos or medical details. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to address the request and any applicable legal obligations; contact us to request deletion. This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose provider may process standard request information such as IP address and browser details under GitHub's privacy statement. That website hosting is separate from in-app movement processing.

Children's privacy

PocketCoach does not knowingly collect children's personal information through the app. If a child sends personal information through a support message, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its removal.

Changes to this policy

If PocketCoach's data practices change, this policy and the relevant App Store privacy or Google Play Data safety information will be updated before those changes are released.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to apov@me.com.
