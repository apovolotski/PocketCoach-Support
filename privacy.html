Privacy Policy

Pocket Coach is designed to work without an account, advertising, analytics, or collection of personal data.

Information stored on your device

The app can store match details, marked errors, scores, notes, optional video links, coach-review previews, and squat workout summaries. This information remains in the app's local storage on your device.

Camera and movement analysis

Squat Coach uses the camera only while a live coaching session is open. Apple's on-device Vision framework identifies body-joint positions from camera frames so the app can count repetitions and provide general form cues. Pocket Coach does not record, save, upload, or share camera frames or movement video. Only a workout summary, such as repetition count and scores, is stored locally.

Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information or usage data. Pocket Coach does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Video links and coach-review preview

Entering a video link does not upload the video or the link to us. The coach-review area is a local preview only; it does not process payments or send information to a coach.

Data removal

You can delete individual matches inside the app. Deleting Pocket Coach from your device removes match data, workout summaries, and other locally stored app data, subject to your device backup settings.

Children's privacy

Because the app does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If Pocket Coach's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before those changes are released.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to apov@me.com.
